faraday-cv

Tracks the pendulum magnet by colour segmentation inside your browser, syncs it to a separately uploaded Arduino voltage log using an LED timing marker, and produces publication-ready figures. The video is never sent to the server — tracking happens on this page; only the physics and the plots are computed server-side.

1. Video

Played and analysed right here on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no size limit and the footage never leaves this browser.

2. Colour range (segmentation)

Click the magnet on the canvas and an HSV range is picked for you; fine-tune it with the sliders. The pixels tinted green are the ones being read as “the magnet”.

If tracking jumps onto something else (skin, background, a reflection), set the furthest the magnet can really travel between frames. Detections beyond that are ignored and the previous position is kept.

3. Reference settings

“Tracking fps” is how many frames per second to step through. Match it to the camera's actual frame rate (if you don't know it, leaving it at 30 is usually fine).
